## PODCASTS

**Andrej Karpathy: From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering** — *Training Data Podcast*

The Takeaway: Andrej Karpathy believes the programming paradigm has fundamentally shifted — vibe coding was just the gateway drug, and now "agentic engineering" is where the real transformation lies.

In a wide-ranging conversation, Karpathy shared his striking admission that he's "never felt more behind as a programmer" — but in a liberating way. He described the December transition where AI models went from producing code that needed constant correction to code that just "came out fine." This triggered his shift from writing code to "vibe coding" (guiding AI with natural language), and now to what he calls "agentic engineering" — setting up systems where AI agents work on complex, multi-step tasks with minimal human intervention.

On the future of programming, Karpathy sees a world where developers become "verification engineers" — not writing code, but specifying intent and validating outputs. He argued that the bottleneck shifts from generation to evaluation: "I'm basically now a reviewer, not a writer." He also pushed back on the idea that AI will make humans obsolete in coding, instead framing it as augmentation: the best engineers will be those who learn to collaborate most effectively with AI, not compete against it.

The conversation also touched on his observations about AI model improvement curves, the role of synthetic data in training, and why he believes we're still early in this transformation.
