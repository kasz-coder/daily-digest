## PODCASTS

**AI & I by Every — "The Secrets of Claude's Platform From the Team Who Built It"**

The Takeaway: Anthropic's platform team is building toward a future where Claude understands itself well enough to dynamically route work, spin up sub-agents, and self-optimize — eliminating the architectural decisions that currently burden developers.

Dan Shipper hosts Angela (Head of Product, Cloud Platform) and Caitlin (Head of Engineering, Cloud Platform) at Anthropic for a deep look at the thinking behind Claude's platform evolution. The conversation centers on a pivotal question: what does "platform" even mean for a model company in 2026?

It started as a completion endpoint. Then tool calling and chat sessions. Now with Cloud Managed Agents, you're essentially getting a cloud on a computer with persistent memory. But the team's ambition goes further: they want Claude to understand itself well enough to figure out which model to use, how to spin up sub-agents, and how to route work — without developers having to architect these decisions themselves.

The vision: "In that world, if Claude is on the fly, your agents on the fly are becoming what they need to become in order for you to do what you're trying to do." The platform has to seriously scale to support this. When AI systems can rewrite their own scaffolding in real time, the infrastructure beneath them becomes the critical bottleneck.

What comes through is a company thinking hard about where the abstraction layer should live — and betting that the answer is "lower than developers currently expect."
