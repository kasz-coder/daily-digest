**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "Why Every AI Agent Needs Its Own Computer | Ivan Burazin (Daytona)"**

The Takeaway: Ivan Burazin, CEO of Daytona, argues that every AI agent will need its own sandboxed computer environment to function effectively, and this is becoming the most critical piece of agent infrastructure.

Ivan Burazin is the CEO of Daytona, one of the most talked-about startups in agent infrastructure. With sixteen years of experience building developer tools, he brings a first-principles perspective on why agents need their own machines. He describes agents as "digital knowledge workers" who, like their human counterparts, need a computer to do anything meaningful.

The conversation tackles a fundamental problem: when you ask an AI to fetch data from your bank, it can't actually log in and access your systems. Giving an agent its own sandbox environment solves this. Burazin argues that every agent will need at least one sandbox, sometimes more, and this is driving a massive shift in infrastructure.

He also explains why Daytona had to throw out Kubernetes and write their own scheduler, and warns that a global CPU shortage might be coming faster than people think. The episode covers the full agent stack as Burazin sees it: models, sandboxes, tools, MCP, memory, orchestration — and where each piece is heading.
