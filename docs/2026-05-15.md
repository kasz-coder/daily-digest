## OFFICIAL BLOGS

**Claude Blog**: "New in Claude Managed Agents: dreaming, outcomes, and multiagent orchestration"

Anthropic launched major updates to Claude Managed Agents. "Dreaming" is a scheduled process where agents review past sessions to find patterns and self-improve — it surfaces recurring mistakes, workflows that converge on, and team preferences. "Outcomes" lets developers write a rubric describing what success looks like; a separate grader evaluates output against criteria so agents can self-correct. Multiagent orchestration lets a lead agent delegate work to specialist subagents with their own models and tools, running in parallel on a shared filesystem.

Internal benchmarks show outcomes improved task success by up to 10 points on the hardest problems, with file generation quality up +8.4% on docx and +10.1% on pptx. Teams like Harvey, Netflix, and Spiral are already using these features.
[https://claude.com/blog/new-in-claude-managed-agents](https://claude.com/blog/new-in-claude-managed-agents)
