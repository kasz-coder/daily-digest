## OFFICIAL BLOGS

### Anthropic Engineering

**An update on recent Claude Code quality reports** — Anthropic traced recent user reports of degraded responses to three separate issues, all now resolved as of April 20 (v2.1.116):

1. On March 4, they changed Claude Code's default reasoning effort from high to medium to reduce latency, but this was the wrong tradeoff. They reverted on April 7 after users preferred higher intelligence.

2. On March 26, a caching optimization designed to clear old thinking after an hour of idle time had a bug — it cleared thinking on every turn instead of just once, making Claude seem forgetful and repetitive. Fixed April 10.

3. On April 16, a system prompt instruction to reduce verbosity ("keep text between tool calls to ≤25 words") hurt coding quality and was reverted April 20.

The aggregate effect looked like broad degradation. They've since reset usage limits for all subscribers and are implementing tighter controls on system prompt changes, including broader eval suites and gradual rollouts.
[https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/april-23-postmortem](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/april-23-postmortem)

**"ElevenLabs' Mati Staniszewski: How Voice Becomes the Interface for Everything"** — ElevenLabs co-founder Mati Staniszewski grew up in Poland where foreign movies were always narrated by a single monotone voice — a terrible experience that sparked his vision: everyone should be able to speak any language with the same emotion and intonation. He and his cofounder (his best friend from high school) started ElevenLabs in 2022 to solve this. The key insight: voice is becoming the default interface for AI, not just a nice-to-have. The company has seen massive adoption — their API went from zero to processing billions of requests as developers realized voice is the natural way to interact with AI systems. They initially faced criticism about potential misuse (deepfakes) but focused on building robust detection tools rather than restricting access. "The biggest risk is not that the technology exists — it's that the people who are building it responsibly don't have a seat at the table."
